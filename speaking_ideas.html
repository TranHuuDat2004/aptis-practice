Quan điểm bản thân

Ngân hàng ý tưởng Speaking - Dạng câu hỏi quan điểm

💡 Cách sử dụng

Khi được hỏi "Why do you like X?" hoặc "What are the benefits of Y?", bạn có thể sử dụng các ý tưởng và mẫu câu dưới đây để phát triển câu trả lời của mình một cách logic và thuyết phục.

💡 Cách vận dụng

Khi nhận được câu hỏi "Why...?", bạn không cần phải nghĩ ra ý tưởng mới hoàn toàn. Thay vào đó, hãy chọn 2-3 ý tưởng từ ngân hàng bên dưới và kết hợp chúng lại thành một câu trả lời hoàn chỉnh.

Ví dụ: "Why do you prefer reading e-books to printed books?"

(Chiến lược: Chọn 3 ý tưởng từ ngân hàng: Time, Money, Convenience)

Point: I prefer reading e-books for several reasons.

Explain 1 (Time): Firstly, it saves me a lot of time. I can download a new book instantly instead of going to a bookstore.

Explain 2 (Money): Secondly, it helps me save money because e-books are often cheaper than printed copies.

Explain 3 (Convenience): Finally, it's very convenient. I can carry thousands of books on my phone, which is easy to access anywhere.

Link: That's why e-books are my favorite choice.

1. Kiến thức / Học hỏi (Knowledge)

  • It helps me learn something new and expand my knowledge.
  • It gives me a sense of growth and confidence.

2. Kỹ năng (Skills)

  • It helps me develop important skills such as communication, teamwork, and creativity.
  • I can become more confident and perform better in different situations.

3. Truyền cảm hứng / Động lực (Inspiration / Motivation)

  • It motivates/inspires me to do my best and improve myself every day.
  • It improves my imagination and creativity, especially when I...
  • It gives me energy and inspiration to move forward, even when things are difficult.

4. Hiệu quả / Năng suất (Productivity)

  • It allows me to work or study more efficiently and save effort.
  • It helps me focus better and avoid distractions.
  • It helps me do things faster with better results.

5. Thời gian (Time)

  • It is fast/ It saves me a lot of time/ It doesn’t take a long time.
  • I can do it quickly and efficiently.

6. Tiền bạc (Money)

  • It helps me save money.
  • It’s affordable and doesn’t cost much.

7. Tiện lợi (Convenience)

  • It’s very convenient and easy to access or use.
  • I don’t have to prepare much or travel far.

8. Chất lượng (Quality)

  • It has good quality, so I can trust it and use it for a long time.
  • When something is well-made or well-organized, I feel more satisfied and comfortable.
  • I prefer things with high quality because they are more reliable and enjoyable.

9. Tự do / Linh hoạt (Freedom / Flexibility)

  • I can do it anytime or anywhere I want, depending on my schedule.
  • It gives me control over my time and choices.

10. An toàn (Safety)

  • It is safe and hygienic, so I don’t have to worry about my health.
  • I feel safer because we can take care of each other and avoid risky situations.
  • It makes me feel protected.

11. Sức khỏe (Health)

  • It’s good for my physical and mental health.
  • It helps me stay active and feel better.
  • It reduces stress and improves my mood / keeps me in good shape.
  • I feel more energetic and positive when I do it regularly.

12. Cảm xúc (Feelings)

  • It makes me feel happy and relaxed, especially after a long day.
  • It helps me release stress and feel more positive.
  • It gives me peace of mind.

13. Giải trí / Vui vẻ (Entertainment / Fun)

  • It’s a fun way to spend my time and relax after a long day.
  • It keeps me entertained and helps me forget my worries.

14. Mối quan hệ (Relationships)

  • It helps me connect with others by spending quality time together.
  • Sharing experiences with people I care about makes me feel connected and loved.
  • It’s a great way to build and maintain strong relationships.
  • It helps me interact with others and build strong connections.

15. Môi trường / Thiên nhiên (Nature / Environment)

  • I can enjoy fresh air and beautiful landscapes.
  • It helps me connect with nature and protect the environment.

16. Thành tựu / Thành công (Achievement / Success)

  • It helps me reach my goals and feel proud of my efforts.
  • Achieving something makes me believe in myself and motivates me to keep improving.
